Privacy policy

Second VR · last updated 2026-10-19

Second VR does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Settings (headset, lens and display options), the list of recently watched videos (file names and where they are) and the NAS server addresses and user names you enter are stored on your iPhone.
  • NAS passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone.
  • None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Network access

The app connects only to the NAS servers (SMB file shares) that you add, to read the videos you choose to play. It finds servers on your local network with Bonjour, which is why iOS asks for Local Network permission. Video data goes directly between your NAS and your iPhone.

Sensors

The app reads the motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) to track your head movement while you watch. This data is used in real time on the device only and is not stored or transmitted. The app does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts or your photos.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
